How to Play

Goal: Good (Loyal Servants + Merlin) need 3 successful quests. Evil (Minions + Assassin) need 3 failed quests, or 5 rejected team proposals, or assassinate Merlin if Good wins. No passing — each player joins on their own device via invite link. Roles stay private on your device.

GOOD

Loyal Servant, Merlin (sees Evil), Percival (sees Merlin)

EVIL

Minion, Assassin, Morgana (fools Percival), Mordred (hidden), Oberon (isolated)

  1. Join — Host taps Play now (new 4-letter code) and shares link. Friends open link or tap Join a friend’s game and enter code. Add bots to reach 5–10.
  2. Roles — Each device shows YOUR ALLEGIANCE privately. Tap I know my part when ready. No passing — your role never leaves your device. Merlin sees Evil (except Mordred), Evil knows each other (except Oberon), Percival sees Merlin/Morgana.
  3. Team Proposal — Leader picks team (size per quest track circles 2 3 2 3 3). Visible to all, timer view-only.
  4. Team Vote — All on their devices tap Approve/Reject simultaneously. Majority approves → quest. Tie or reject → next leader, ✕ tracker +1. Five rejects → Evil wins.
  5. Quest — Team members on their devices tap Succeed (Good must) or Fail (Evil may). 1 Fail fails quest — Quest 4 with 7+ needs 2.
  6. Assassination — If Good gets 3 quests, Assassin (on their device) names Merlin. Correct → Evil steals win.

Quest sizes: 5p [2,3,2,3,3] • 6p [2,3,4,3,4] • 7p [2,3,3,4*,4] • 8-10p [3,4,4,5*,5] — * needs 2 fails. Tracker resets after each quest.

Tip: Invite link looks like ?room=ABCD (your 4-letter code) — each player’s role/votes are per-device, never leaked to others or logs.
